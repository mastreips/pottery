FERRIC CHLORIDE REDUCTION

FERRIC CHLORIDE is extremely toxic, especially in aerosol form, so be aware that gloves and a respirator are definitely part of the process. Much like PITFIRE, FERRIC CHLORIDE provides a great background color (pinkish red) and is further enhanced using nuts, salt, chlorides and discarded fruits and vegetables.
