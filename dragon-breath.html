DRAGON'S BREATH

DRAGON'S BREATH is essentially the same process as RAKU. The difference lies in the firing temperature, varying the temperature +/- 2-300 degrees (F) the results take on a different texture. This is highly experimental and honestly produces its share of failures, but the successful results can be stunning.
